{{<indentline>}} With the hostile rebellion force in orbit, and a likely extermination of all Federation members of this facility, Dr. Rae and her team were frantically gathering

{{<indentline>}} _How did they find us?_ The thought was stabbing at the back of Angela's mind, while she frantically navigatated authorization steps of top secret subprocesses. _This moon isn't even registered in the Federation Naval Charts - it doesn't exist. Do we have a spy on our team?_

{{<indentline>}} The team's predicament, Angela realised, was the delicate challenge of keeping top secret research out of Rebel hands, until Federation forces return on their scheduled re-supply mission. On the flip side, locking the research away for an indefinite time would place it too far out of Federation scientist's reach, in a war that the Federation is currently losing.  
{{<indentline>}} What was needed, which some Federation geniuses on Earth had realised upon building the the station,  was technology to lock away invaluable research secrets for just enough time until Federation forces would return and retake control of the facility.  
What was needed, was to send a message into the future.
