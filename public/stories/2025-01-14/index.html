Survival of the Weakest

January 14, 2025   ·   Written by CryptoPatrick   ·   Reading time 2 min

⠀⠀⠀⠀⠀ “Sir, I’m sorry to bother you at this hour, but you need to see this.”
⠀⠀⠀⠀⠀ I looked at the young Staff Sargent, clearly distressed from having to wake an American Star Fleet Admiral in the middle of the night. I made a gesture for him to lead the way. The Staff Sargent flinched. I was just about to utter words, when I realized that I was still wearing the blue bathrobe that my wife had got me for Christmas.
⠀⠀⠀⠀⠀ “Give me a minute,” I almost blushed, and as I closed the door to my Officer qaurters, a sense of relieve swept accross the Staff Sargent’s face.
⠀⠀⠀⠀⠀ A few minutes later, as we entered the Command Room, I was struck by the eerie silence of the normally busy room - an outlier event was clearly in progress.
⠀⠀⠀⠀⠀ “Report!” I snapped, and the room came to attention, as every pair of eyes turned towards me.
⠀⠀⠀⠀⠀ “At ease! Kenneth - what do we got?”.
⠀⠀⠀⠀⠀ The smartest human being in the room, Kenneth Galbraith, an Officer who had refused to rise through the ranks, despite our attempts to promote him, took a step forward and thundered with his formal voice…
⠀⠀⠀⠀⠀ “Sir!! Approximately seven hours ago our instruments registered unusual seismic activity . . .,” I raised a hand to cut him off.
⠀⠀⠀⠀⠀ “Don’t give me the story. Just tell me what I need to know.” The slightest of smiles swept accross Kenneth’s face, relieved of being spared the grueling task of giving a Full Situational Report.
⠀⠀⠀⠀⠀ “Sir! One of our mining robots on TEL8-NINE signaled that it was being scanned by an orbiting spaceborn vessel of unknown origin. 200 milliseconds later we lost contact with the robot. We believe a hostile act was the reason.”
⠀⠀⠀⠀⠀ The normally calm and collected officer was clearly distressed by something. But whatever it was, he had left it out of the report.

Three robots face off on a planet. Each robot is running simulation on which enemy to attack. We follow on millisecond granularity (with busses, caches, and electrons, cosmic radiation, etc) how the robots think in the truel, and how the weakest of the three robots (of different nationalities) end up winning.

The Robots have shields, limited ammunition, and varying processing power. Can they enter some kind of livelock or deadlock?
